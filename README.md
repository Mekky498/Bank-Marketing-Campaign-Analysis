# 🏦 Bank Marketing Campaign Analysis & Customer Segmentation

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze direct bank marketing campaign performance and evaluate customer subscription patterns for term deposits.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary

This project evaluates marketing phone campaign efficiency using demographic, socio-economic, and direct contact data. The analysis provides key insights into conversion drivers, segment responsiveness, and optimal communication channels to maximize term deposit subscriptions.

### 🌟 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Contacts:** 11,000
* **Total Subscribers:** 5,000
* **Conversion Rate:** 47.38%
* **Avg Calls per Subscriber:** 5.3
* **Avg Call Duration:** 6.2 minutes

## 🔍 Key Insights & Findings

* **Job Role Performance:** Students (74.72%) and Retirees (66.32%) exhibited the highest subscription rates among all demographic groups.
* **Age Segmentation:** Clients aged **60+** showed the strongest response rate at **82.16%**.
* **Communication Channel:** **Cellular** contacts achieved a significantly higher conversion rate (**54.33%**) compared to telephone or unknown channels.
* **Call Duration:** Longer call durations directly correlate with higher subscription rates, with converted calls averaging **6.2 minutes**.
